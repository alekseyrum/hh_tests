Choose the correct option to complete the sentence
All the marketing team is in the conference room. They ____ the new campaing strategy.

правильный ответ:
++		: must be brainstorming

ошибки:
-- could brainstorm
-- can be brainstormed 
-- should brainstorm
-- might have brainstormed

* модальный глагол must в сочетании с длительным инфинитивом (be + глагол с -ing). Такая конструкция выражает высокую степень уверенности (логическое предположение) о действии, которое происходит прямо сейчас, в момент речи.

Read the text and answer the question

Development of Soft Skills
Soft skills, also known as interpersonal skills, are the abilities we use to work and communicate with other people. They include skills like clear communication, good teamwork, being adaptable, solving problems and understanding emotions. In today's workplace employers value these skills highly. Soft skills work together with technical knowledge and help both people and companies succeed.
Technical skills are necessary to do specific job tasks. However, soft skills allow employees to use their technical knowledge in the best way. For example, communicating clearly helps everyone understand ideas, which reduces mistakes and saves time. Good teamwork brings together people with different strengths to achieve shared goals. Being adaptable helps people manage changes at work, like new software or different market conditions, while staying productive.
Developing soft skills has many professional benefits. People with strong soft skills are usually better at resolving conflicts. They can build stronger relationships with coworkers and clients and they are more likely to be chosen for leadership positions. Also, as machines and computers handle simple tasks, human skills like creavity, empathy and critical thinking are becoming more important than ever.
You can improve your soft skills with focused effort and practice. Useful strategies include asking for feedback, learning from good examples, joining training courses, and thinking about your daily interactions. It is also helpful to try new challenges that push you out of your comfort zone, such as working on a big team project. Needless too say, working on soft skills is a smart investment.
